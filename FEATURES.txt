HARDWARE STORE CASH BOOK - FEATURES
===================================

A simple, offline cash book for a hardware shop.
Works without internet. Nothing to install. Amounts are shown in Pakistani Rupees (Rs. 125,000).
Stock: you can keep a simple stock list (see section 8). It is separate from the cash book.


1. LOGIN AND SECURITY
---------------------
* First start: the owner (admin) creates the account - shop name, name, username, password.
* A Recovery Key is shown once, to reset the owner password if it is forgotten.
* Every later start asks for username and password.
* Two kinds of users:
    Owner - can do everything, including Settings, Backup & Restore, and adding staff.
    Staff - can record entries and see reports, but cannot open Settings or Backup & Restore.
* Owner can add staff, reset a staff password or remove a staff user.
* Every user can change their own password.
* After 5 wrong passwords, login waits 30 seconds.
* Passwords are stored scrambled (salted SHA-256), never as plain text.


2. DASHBOARD
------------
* Today's income, today's expenses, customer credit, supplier payable and cash balance.
  
* Quick buttons to add income, expense, customer, payment, loan or product.
* List of the most recent transactions.
* Reminder if automatic backup is off or needs permission.


3. INCOME / SALES
-----------------
* Record each sale with date, amount, description and payment method.
* Payment methods: Cash or Credit (on account).
* Credit sales are linked to a customer and added to what he owes.
* Search, filter by date, edit and delete entries.


4. CUSTOMERS
------------
* Customer list with name, phone and address.
* Credit given and payments received for each customer.
* Running balance: how much each customer still owes.
* Full statement for each customer, with print.
* A customer with saved records cannot be deleted by mistake.


5. SUPPLIERS
------------
* Supplier list with name, phone and address.
* Record supplier bills (amount you owe) and payments made.
* Running balance: how much you still owe each supplier.
* Full statement for each supplier, with print.


6. EXPENSES
-----------
* Record shop expenses by category: Rent, Electricity, Salary, Transport, Food, Repairs, Shop Maintenance, Miscellaneous.
* Search, filter by category and date, edit and delete.


7. LOANS
--------
* Loans you took (borrowed) and loans you gave.
* Repayments recorded against each loan.
* Shows the paid amount and the remaining balance.


8. STOCK (products, sold, remaining)
------------------------------------
* Add each product with its name, unit (pcs, kg, bag, box, feet, meter, litre ...),
  the stock you have in the store now, "Order When Stock Falls To" (alert level) and the
  "Usual Order Quantity" (both optional).
* "+ Add Stock" when new goods arrive. "Sold" when goods are sold.
* The software shows, for every product: Opening, Stock In, Sold and Remaining.
  Remaining = Opening + Stock In - Sold.
* Status for each product: In Stock, Low Stock or Out of Stock.
* ITEMS TO ORDER list: shows every product that is finished or has fallen to its order
  level, with the usual quantity to order. Print Order List (or Order List PDF) to take it
  to the supplier. A product leaves the list when you add stock.
* It does not let you sell more than what is in the store.
* Stock History lists every entry. Filter by product, type or date. Edit or delete entries.
* Search products by name.
* Dashboard shows a Stock alert when a product is low or finished.
* Print Stock Report / Order List, or download them as PDF, with the shop header and signature.
* Stock is separate from the cash book: recording stock does not change any money.
  Record the money for a sale in Income / Sales as usual.
* Stock is saved and backed up with all other records.


9. CASH BOOK
------------
* Automatic cash balance: opening cash + money in - money out.
* Day-by-day list of every cash movement with running balance.
* Choose any period: today, yesterday, this week, this month or custom dates.
* Print or download as PDF.


10. REPORTS
----------
* Choose a period: today, yesterday, this week, this month or custom dates.
* Totals for income, expenses, credit given, payments received, supplier payments and loans.
* "Net Recorded Income" = Income minus recorded Expenses
  (it is not true profit, because the cost of goods is not recorded).
* Print Report - prints a professional A4 report.
* Download Report (PDF) - saves the same report as a PDF file to keep or send.
* Printed report includes:
    - Shop name, address and phone number at the top of every page
    - Page number ("Page 1 of 3") at the bottom of every page
    - Signature section at the bottom of the last page
* The owner can edit the shop address and phone number (Reports page or Settings).


11. AUTOMATIC SAVING AND BACKUP
-------------------------------
* Every change is saved at once inside the browser - no Save button is needed.
* A second hidden copy is kept inside the browser and is used automatically
  if the main copy is ever lost.
* Automatic Backup to a folder (Chrome / Edge): the owner chooses a folder once, and the
  software then writes a backup file there about one second after every change.
  A dated copy is kept for each of the last 14 days.
* A small indicator in the menu shows whether automatic backup is ON.
* Manual backup: Download Backup saves everything to one file (.json).
* Restore Backup: bring all records back from a backup file. It also works on the
  first-start screen, for a new computer.


12. SETTINGS (owner only)
-------------------------
* Shop name, address and phone number.
* Opening cash balance.


13. GENERAL
-----------
* Works completely offline. No internet, no account, no monthly fee.
* No installation: unzip and open index.html.
* Works in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge (best) on Windows.
* Layout fits a computer, tablet or phone screen.
* Simple, clean, professional design.
* Amounts always shown in PKR format.
* Confirmation before deleting anything.
* Checks all input (no empty or wrong amounts).


14. GOOD TO KNOW
----------------
* Records are saved inside the browser on that computer. Always use the same browser and
  keep the software in the same folder.
* Do not clear browser "site data / cookies", as this can delete the records.
  (Automatic Backup protects you from this - turn it on.)
* Urdu letters cannot be shown in the downloaded PDF. Use Print > Save as PDF for Urdu text.
* Page numbers on printed pages need Chrome or Edge version 131 or newer.
